# BlackShark Action Planning

Turn an agreed direction into work people can execute. This is the general method owned and maintained by 黑鲨（BlackShark）; it does not require a particular repository, organization, or collaboration platform.

## Inherit before planning

Carry forward the supplied objective, desired customer behavior, operating constraints, confirmed decisions, delegated authority, owners, and explicit non-goals. Label facts, decisions, proposals, and unknowns separately. Do not turn an unknown execution detail into a reason to reopen the direction: within existing authority, continue with a bounded draft and mark only the affected task as not yet ready. Do not ask for a confirmed item to be approved again; in other words, do not reapprove（不重新审批）it unless a material premise, commitment, or decision boundary changed.

A simple task（简单任务）that is already approved, low-risk, reversible, and owned may go directly to execution on its existing carrier. Do not create a project, meeting, or approval merely because this Skill was invoked.

## Work through the unfinished thinking

Match the interaction to the user's state. If the work is still being figured out, sketch its main work blocks and develop the current block together. If the user has already supplied sufficient decisions, produce the requested plan or handoff directly. Do not force a discovery interview before every plan.

For a vague block such as “make the page” or “coordinate the launch,” identify the unresolved decision that most changes the work: what must be true, what input is needed, who can supply it, and what happens next. Read supplied materials first. Ask concrete questions about that dependency, explain which action the answer changes, and offer bounded options when helpful. Do not dump a generic questionnaire, fix the number of questions, or make the user repeat settled details. Continue independent preparation while answers are pending.

After an answer, revise the affected actions, dependencies, handoffs, and workload. Separate the user's decision from an AI proposal; do not merely append the answer below an unchanged task list. Make “obtain input,” “make the decision,” and “produce the deliverable” visible when each requires distinct work. A needed decision is itself work: name the inputs, deciding role, required output, and latest useful moment, rather than leaving a bare “TBD.” Keep professional choices delegated to their owner when the user intends that autonomy.

## Choose useful granularity

Each action should let its recipient understand **why it matters, where to start, which judgment or operation is needed, and what to hand over for whom to use**. Its purpose must connect to an agreed outcome, a necessary dependency, or an uncertainty worth resolving. “Improve results” pasted into every row is not a purpose. If removing a proposed action changes none of those, remove it or justify its cost as an optional proposal.

Split work when a different accountable owner, independently timed input, decision, handoff, or completion check would otherwise be hidden. Keep continuous steps by one owner together when they can be scheduled and accepted as one unit. Use substeps inside a task when that is enough; more rows are not inherently better. Stop splitting when the intended recipient can begin and finish without inventing a missing business decision or handoff. Standard craft techniques and clicks need not become tasks unless the user needs training or an SOP.

For collaborative decomposition, multiple owners, or competing demands, read `references/action-structure.md`. It includes concrete questions, split/stop examples, and workload checks. These are reasoning aids, not mandatory fields or a fixed project template.

## Build the action system

1. Restate the outcome and the observable customer or operating behavior it should change.
2. Enumerate the complete dependency set（完整依赖）needed to reach that outcome, including preparation, execution, protection, evidence collection, and closeout. Do not truncate（不得截断）required work to fit a display limit.
3. Mark dependencies, conflicts, sequencing, parallel work, and any protected operation that must not be harmed.
4. Give each action only the fields it needs to be executable: action and purpose, object or scenario, starting input and key work, responsible owner, deliverable and recipient, due date or window, dependencies, acceptance or evidence, and relevant stop or escalation condition. Purposes may be inherited from a clearly linked work block; do not repeat them mechanically. Preserve unknowns instead of inventing people or dates. Where names are not assigned, use responsible roles and identify how staffing will be resolved; do not assign all execution to the coordinator.
5. Produce a priority view（优先视图）for attention, while retaining the complete action and dependency set. “Top items” are a view, not a cap on total work.
6. Decide the smallest handoff shape: direct execution note for a simple task, coordinated handoff for shared work, or a project package only when complexity, risk, or independent gates justify it.

Before scheduling, walk the necessary chain from preparation to delivery and actual use. Include coordination, revision, follow-up, and closure where they consume work. Check relevant handoff failures; an end-to-end trial is useful for a new multi-party customer path, not a mandatory extra project for every change.

When timing or workload is requested, reverse-plan from the supplied milestones through real dependencies. Distinguish confirmed commitments, proposed dates, and conditional windows; external waits are not guaranteed durations. Separate active effort, elapsed waiting, and ongoing work by its actual frequency or quantity. Label estimates and their scope assumptions; never present them as measured productivity. Check parallel tasks against the same person's capacity, count shared effort once, and preserve the user's staffing and holiday decisions. A full-looking calendar with an impossible dependency is not executable.

## Before handing over a complete plan

For a complete operating plan, a revision after "too vague" feedback, or a plan entering a shared execution system, read the existing [content handoff check](references/action-structure.md#交付前的内容检查) and apply it to the actual draft. An agreed direction does not establish that the actions are ready. Check the source-to-plan content and the real handoffs before treating a task list as executable. An already concrete simple edit needs only a local check.

## Output

Return, at the depth the task needs:

- inherited objective, authority, decisions, and non-goals;
- complete action and dependency set;
- priority view and sequencing;
- ownership, deliverables, timing, acceptance evidence, and stop conditions;
- unresolved details and exactly what they block;
- recommended handoff shape.

Missing commercial evidence does not prevent planning, but it does prevent claims of business effect. Do not equate publication, activity completion, or a filled template with customer behavior, profit, or method reliability.

Use the user's existing carrier where possible. A compact task can read: “To enable [specific use], [role] takes [input], does/decides [work], and gives [output] to [recipient] by [window]; complete when [observable check].” Omit irrelevant parts and use a table only when comparison helps. Detailed discussion notes need not all be copied into the team's handoff.

## Purpose, output and use

每个工作块接通经营目的→本期希望改变什么→做法与假设→当前关键产出→实际使用→结果与下一次选择。继承来源的目的及证据边界，不能把活动名当目的。关键产出要有实际内容、接收者/用途、必需准确性与限制、依赖/岗位/条件排期、实际版本的完成凭证及后续观察。按委托粒度完成关键业务思考，同时保留专业岗位自主；共享产出只计一次准备，不重复算收益。交付合格、能用、实际用了、顾客变化与经营值得分别判断；局部未知不冻结独立工作。

当候选承诺与商品选择范围、总费用、时段、服务或空间不匹配时，先把适配或变更决定接入工作；仅完成说明不能算提供物适配。目的、主次或投入调整后，同步修受影响产出、接收者和验收，保留独立有效工作，不只改项目标题。
